Q4: Count Coins

Given a positive integer total, count the ways to make that amount using 1, 5, 10, and 25 cent coins. Repetition is allowed and the order of coins does not matter.

There are six ways to make 15 cents:

  • 15 pennies
  • 10 pennies and 1 nickel
  • 5 pennies and 2 nickels
  • 5 pennies and 1 dime
  • 3 nickels
  • 1 nickel and 1 dime

Use next_smaller_coin in your recursive solution. It maps 25 → 10 → 5 → 1 → None. You may write a helper to keep track of more than one changing value.

Before you start

Think about what the remaining amount means, and which denominations are still available. Distinguish an exact fit from an impossible branch.
